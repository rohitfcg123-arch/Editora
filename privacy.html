MailBlaster Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MailBlaster (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the MailBlaster bulk email sending website hosted at rohitfcg123-arch.github.io/Editora.

1. Information processed

  • Google account and authorization: If you choose Connect Gmail, Google may show your account identity and request permission for MailBlaster to send email through the Gmail API on your behalf.
  • Contact list and message content: The recipient addresses, names, subject lines, and message text you provide are processed in your browser to prepare and send the messages you request.
  • Campaign status: The page may display sending progress and success or failure results while you use it.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the functions you initiate, including preparing recipient-specific messages and submitting them to Gmail. Email is sent from the Google account you authorize. Do not upload contact lists or send messages unless you have an appropriate lawful basis and permission to contact those recipients.

3. Storage and sharing

The current browser-based version is designed to process contact data in your browser and use Google's Gmail API to submit messages. It does not intentionally upload your contact CSV or message list to a MailBlaster-operated server. The Google authorization token is held in browser memory while the page is in use. Your browser, hosting provider, and Google may process technical data under their own terms and policies. Once submitted, messages are handled by Google and email recipients' providers.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use contact lists for advertising. Do not assume campaign data will be recoverable after you close or refresh the page.

4. Google services

Use of Google sign-in, authorization, and Gmail is subject to Google's terms and privacy disclosures. You can revoke MailBlaster's access in your Google Account security settings. MailBlaster uses Gmail permissions only to provide the email-sending feature you request.

5. Retention and security

Contact and message data used by this version is intended to remain in the active browser session rather than being saved to a MailBlaster server. No website or transmission method is completely secure; protect your device and do not share OAuth tokens or account credentials.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to connect Gmail, remove imported contacts from the page, close the site, or revoke Google access. To ask a privacy question, contact abhyas.app6@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

MailBlaster is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Please review this page periodically.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: abhyas.app6@gmail.com.

Important: This policy describes the current intended browser-based MVP. Review it and make sure it accurately reflects the deployed code and your actual practices before publishing or submitting the app for Google verification.
